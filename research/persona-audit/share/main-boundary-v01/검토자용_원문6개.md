# 발화 연결 검토용 워크시트 · 개발 사례

각 문항은 질문과 가상 화자의 답변입니다. 각 답변을 따로 읽고, **각각 뜻이 통하는 인접한 두 구절 사이에 내용상 연결이 불분명한 곳이 있는지** 검토해 주세요. 질환·중증도나 화자의 의도는 평가하지 않습니다.

명확한 곳이 있으면 ‘있음’과 인접한 원문 두 구절·이유를 적습니다. 해석 가능한 전환이 모두 설명되면 ‘없음’, 연결된다는 해석과 끊긴다는 해석이 모두 가능하면 ‘불확실’과 두 해석을 남깁니다. 텍스트 자체로 관계를 평가할 수 없으면 ‘판정 불가’로 표시합니다. 질문 답변이 늦거나 소재가 바뀌었다는 사실만으로 ‘있음’으로 판정하지 않습니다. 한 문장 안에서도 인접한 두 절을 검토할 수 있습니다. 처음 읽은 판정은 별도 사본에 기록해 보존한 후 토론합니다.

## K01

**질문**: Tell me about your usual morning, from waking up until you get to work.

I usually wake up around seven, maybe a bit later if I’ve had a rough night. I make myself a cup of tea and some toast, then feed the cat before getting dressed. I take the bus to work, so I leave the house just after eight. My sister lives with me, and she’s usually still asleep when I get up. I don’t mind the routine—it helps me feel more in control. Sometimes I hear a voice when I’m alone, but it doesn’t happen every day. I try not to think about it too much. The bus ride is quiet, and I like to read a bit on the way. I usually get to work around nine, and my colleague helps me open the shop. It’s a simple day, but it keeps me going.

## K03

**질문**: Tell me about what you do at the bookshop, from opening until the end of your shift.

I get there around seven thirty, usually. My colleague and I open the shop together—check the locks, turn on the lights, make sure the display shelves look neat. Then I spend most of the day restocking, helping customers find books, and tidying up. There's not too much else to do really. I like the quiet of it, actually. It helps me focus. Sometimes, when it's really still in the shop, I think I hear a voice, but I can't be sure. It's not every day, just now and then. I just try to keep going with my work.

**질문**: Tell me about what you do at the bookshop, from opening until the end of your shift.

Some days I feel more motivated than others. There's a quiet part of the shop where I like to sit and read when there's not much happening. I don't know, sometimes I think I hear a voice in the back room, but I'm not sure if it's real or just my imagination. I try not to let it bother me too much. The rest of the day is pretty routine—helping customers, restocking, closing up. I go home, make dinner, and watch some TV.
